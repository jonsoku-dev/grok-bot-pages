# no-ai-slop Korean adaptation

This repository adapts the prose principles from Peter Yang's [`no-ai-slop`](https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop) at commit `d30eddb9e04562234f2070b5ee63ca4649d9a05e` under the MIT License (copyright Peter Yang, 2026).

The upstream plugin is not installed, executed, globally registered, or vendored. Its full Skill is larger than the observed safe Grok Description budget and is not Grok-specific. The repository instead keeps a compact Korean `human_readable_prose_only` policy in `config/policies.yaml`.

The adaptation preserves facts, numbers, uncertainty, citations, personality, cadence, structured contracts, code, commands, URLs, proper nouns, original titles, and required quotations. It favors direct, concrete, natural Korean and removes empty openings, importance puffery, forced binary contrasts, faux insights, unnamed attribution, synonym cycling, robotic rhythm, fake-profound endings, and redundant recaps when they add no meaning.

Edit reports and slop-detection reports are opt-in. Normal Bot responses should not append ceremonial "what changed" sections or style diagnostics.
